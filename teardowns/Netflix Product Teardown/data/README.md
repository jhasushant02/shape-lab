# Data

This teardown uses no raw dataset. All figures were gathered from public sources and
compiled into the deck in `analysis/NETFLIX_Product_Teardown.pdf`.

## Sources

| Used for | Source |
|----------|--------|
| Company overview, business model, mission, USP | Netflix website |
| App rating and user-review pain points | Google Play Store |
| Subscriber, revenue and market-share statistics | Statista, LinkedIn articles |
| Competitor users, market share and features | Statista, competitor (OTT) websites |

## Notes

- Figures (users, market share, revenue, pricing) are as of the deck's publication
  and have not been re-verified since. Re-check before reusing them.
- The user personas are illustrative archetypes, not drawn from interview data.
